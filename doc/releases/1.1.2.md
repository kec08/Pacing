# Pacing 1.1.2

> 2026-07-31 main 반영 ([#83](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/83)) · 저장소에 기록된 첫 릴리스

Pacing의 기본 기능을 담은 버전입니다. 이 버전까지의 개발 내용을 한곳에 정리했습니다.

- **기본 탭**: 온보딩, 홈, 러닝, 마이 탭과 친구 탭을 만들었습니다. ([#2](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/2), [#4](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/4), [#6](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/6), [#8](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/8), [#25](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/25))
- **계정**: Firebase 인증과 Firestore 저장, Apple·Google·카카오·네이버·이메일 로그인, 프로필 사진과 회원가입 흐름을 넣었습니다. 계정 삭제도 지원합니다. ([#12](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/12), [#17](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/17), [#21](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/21), [#22](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/22), [#63](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/63), [#65](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/65))
- **러닝 기록**: 러닝 경로, 페이스와 종료 요약, 백그라운드 측정, 활동 상세의 경로·구간 페이스를 제공합니다. ([#33](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/33), [#37](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/37), [#38](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/38), [#42](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/42), [#47](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/47), [#50](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/50), [#58](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/58))
- **Apple Music**: 플레이리스트 연동, Apple Music 스타일 플레이어, 노래 탭을 넣었습니다. ([#13](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/13), [#20](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/20), [#41](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/41), [#46](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/46), [#48](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/48))
- **같이 듣기·주변 러너**: 주변 러너를 지도에서 찾고 같은 음악을 함께 들을 수 있습니다. ([#15](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/15), [#19](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/19), [#31](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/31), [#52](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/52), [#68](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/68))
- **친구·프로필**: 친구 프로필 상세, 프로필 편집, 친구 위치와 최근 음악, 프로필 활동 표시를 넣었습니다. ([#27](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/27), [#35](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/35), [#40](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/40), [#71](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/71), [#73](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/73), [#75](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/75), [#79](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/79), [#83](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/83))
- **앱 품질**: 스켈레톤 UI, Pacing 브랜드 UI, 개인정보 처리방침, Firestore 보안 규칙을 적용했습니다. ([#29](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/29), [#54](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/54), [#56](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/56), [#66](https://github.com/kec08/Pacing/pull/66))
